## Export specification

- MP4, H.264 High, yuv420p, 1080 × 1920, 9:16, 30 fps.
- AAC stereo, 48 kHz, 256 kbps. Music is ducked below narration.
- Key text is placed inside the central mobile viewing area; browser chrome, desktop file paths, bookmarks, and account initials are excluded.
- Both cuts include large burned-in captions. The shorter cut compresses the middle workflow and ends with a three-second CTA.

Editable timing maps and narration WAV files are included. The rendering source and intermediate assets remain in the parent ad-work folder for future changes.

## Verification

Both MP4s passed a complete audio/video decode. The main cut measured −16.1 LUFS integrated loudness and −2.6 dBFS true peak; the short cut measured −16.1 LUFS and −2.0 dBFS true peak. Neither clips. Full-duration visual filmstrips were reviewed, and browser playback was checked with mute enabled and disabled. The final hook holds on the website entry card to exclude the raw recording’s early scroll into other tools.

This environment cannot provide an audible listening review to the editor. Narration was checked through its script, duration, decoded signal, playback state, and volume measurements; its pronunciation and perceived naturalness still need a human listening pass.
